IGNITION
Morning Setup

Unity skill playing on loop all night. Waking into the material rather than cold-starting. The first move after eyes open is not gaming — it's music. Get the electricity running through beats first, then everything downstream flows.

Gaming is allowed to jumpstart the brain if needed, but music is the true morning anchor. That's the correction: beats before play, always.

SEQUENCE
The Daily Stream Order

YouTube live. Ableton or 404A. Beats exported → Box.com → auto-sync to Freestyle King site. People react most to this. Lead with the thing that lands.

Start cooking on camera. Hold the energy. When the live fills up and people start joining — that's the cue. Drop the spatula, hit the exercises. The audience triggers the workout. Brilliant method.
