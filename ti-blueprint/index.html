The Core Thesis
The Manifesto

The tech job is not the absolute best solution. It is only valuable if you need it — and that realization alone is an epiphany that most people never arrive at. The woman who just runs her own thing, makes her own money off her platform, and gets very good at AI — she is maximizing her human potential. Why not that? Why not exactly that?


You are making yourself the most versatile version of yourself. Absolute dominance over your own life. The only variable is the people you pick up — and you're already filtering on that. Computer productivity. The body. The assets. These are the three. The body is the sweet spot because that's where music discovery happens. That's where stage presence lives. Stage presence is where conversions happen.


If you simply run Transparency Interactive as if it's a competing corporation — not to Microsoft, not to Apple, but to Epic Games — and the better it's run, the more powerful it becomes. You have to maintain your body. Your muscles will fatigue. There's no way around it. But the coding with speaking, the AI pipeline, the storyboarding — this is what makes you a highly desired creative software developer. Asset replacement. JavaScript refactoring. Storyboarding. That is the career. That is the path.
