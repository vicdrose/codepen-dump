Asia Kitchen Strategy

The missing American asset is the kitchen. Not freedom. Not adventure. The kitchen. Van life sounds romantic but the hard part is food preparation infrastructure. Asia solves this as a cost-optimization move, not a personality statement.

The calculus: If domestic burn rate exceeds $1,200/month without a stable kitchen, Thailand or Philippines delivers more stability for $300/month — with kitchen, WiFi, and a lower-stress environment to build Transparency Tech deliverables.

Learn the language. If you go, commit to at least conversational Thai or Tagalog. This transforms it from tourism to genuine cost arbitrage. The digital work (Transparency Tech, Astrobeats, Rapbot) runs identically from Chiang Mai as it does from an American city.

Asia is not the dream. Asia is the buffer that protects the dream while assets are still being built domestically.
